    Trotz(despite) + Genetive\\
    ex: Trotz des Regens gehe ich spazieren: Despite the rain, I go for a walk.\\
    ex: Trotz seiner Müdigkeit arbeitet er weiter: Despite his tiredness, he keeps working.\\
    
    \textbf{obwohl} (Konjunktion) -- although / even though\\
    Beispiel: Obwohl es regnet, gehe ich spazieren.\\
    Meaning: Although it is raining, I go for a walk.
    
    \bigskip
    
    \textbf{dennoch} (Adverb) -- nevertheless\\
    Beispiel: Es war kalt, dennoch gingen wir schwimmen.\\
    Meaning: It was cold, nevertheless we went swimming.
    
    \bigskip
    
    \textbf{allerdings} (Adverb) -- however\\
    Beispiel: Das Buch ist interessant, allerdings sehr teuer.\\
    Meaning: The book is interesting, however very expensive.
    
    \bigskip
    
    \textbf{hingegen} (Adverb) -- in contrast / on the other hand\\
    Beispiel: Ich mag Kaffee, sie hingegen trinkt lieber Tee.\\
    Meaning: I like coffee, she on the other hand prefers tea.
